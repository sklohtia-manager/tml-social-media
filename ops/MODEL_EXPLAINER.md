# Model explainer: "Income vs net wealth" (owner-approved design direction, 27 Nov 2026)

Use this as the pattern for future explainers. The live 27 Nov post stays as published; the revised version and the evergreen posts stay pending the owner's approval.

**Graphic (`tools/render.py`)**
- Small kicker (for example MONEY BASICS), then the actual topic as the headline.
- Short definition blocks (term in gold, one plain sentence beneath).
- One highlighted takeaway with a gold bar.
- Source and date on the image whenever a definition or figure comes from a publication, then the disclaimer. Hypothetical examples are labelled.
- Green-and-gold layout and the TML logo, unchanged. Body text 30 px or more.

**Caption pattern**
1. One-line framing of the topic's question.
2. The definitions, attributed to the source in plain words.
3. The takeaway, then a labelled hypothetical example if useful.
4. Source line (with link on Facebook and X), then "Learn. Understand. Stay Informed." and the disclaimer.
5. Hashtags: #TwoMarketLedger #TMLEducation plus topic tags.
6. Close on the topic's own takeaway. The optional signature line goes in only when the topic naturally connects to preparedness, resilience or planning ahead (once, second-last body line, with lead-in and closing), together with #AreYouFinanciallyReady.
