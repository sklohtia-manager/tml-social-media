# Two Market Ledger: authoritative publishing and editorial policy (v3, 28 November 2026)

**Owner directives, 28 Nov 2026 (daily publishing; global news coverage).** This is the single policy for the three daily editions (9:30 AM Morning, 3:15 PM Ready/Education, 7:30 PM Evening; Australia/Melbourne, every day including weekends and public holidays) and for every platform and workflow that researches or publishes news or education. Where any other file conflicts with it, **this file wins**; the founding principles and the compliance rules in `policy/FINANCIAL_CONTENT_RULES.md` (as amended by the 28 Nov directives) still apply in full. An edition is complete only when it is scheduled or published to Instagram, Facebook and X **and its status is verified**.

## 0. What Two Market Ledger is
A **global** financial, economic, business and technology news and educational platform, with particular relevance to Australian and Indian audiences. It is **not** an Australian-only government-announcement service. Daily research is not restricted to ABS, RBA, Treasury, RBI or other government releases. The purpose is to help ordinary people understand what is happening around the world and how it may affect economies, businesses, employment, markets and everyday life. Stories are chosen for economic and public relevance, not for the country where the event occurred. Both positive and negative developments are covered; do not run only recession, inflation, war or hardship stories.

## 1. Geography and categories
**Regions:** Australia and New Zealand; India and South Asia; United States and Canada; United Kingdom and Europe; Middle East; Russia and surrounding regions; China, Japan and South Korea; Southeast Asia; other economically significant regions.
**Approved daily topics:** inflation and cost of living; interest rates and central-bank decisions; oil, petrol, diesel, gas and electricity prices; international energy supplies; wars and geopolitical developments with economic consequences; sanctions and tariffs; shipping and supply-chain disruption; technology and AI; major technology-company announcements; global technology outages; cybersecurity incidents affecting businesses and consumers; employment, wages and job losses; banking and financial services; global stock-market developments; gold, commodities and currency movements; food prices and agriculture; property and housing affordability; government economic policy; major corporate announcements, earnings and restructuring; international trade and growth; significant consumer and business developments.
Each item in the research record carries one of these as its `category`.

## 2. Content ladder (move down automatically; reserve HOLD for genuine issues)
| Tier | Content | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| A | Latest verified news from anywhere relevant: a credible report or official release from the last 24 hours (Morning: since the previous Evening edition). **If there is no major Australian item, use international news.** | "News"; only tier A may say new, latest or today |
| B | Recent verified development from the previous 24–72 hours (or a recent verified economic development), clearly dated | Date in the first line; "In case you missed it"; never "breaking" or "today" |
| C | Market summary or weekly economic and business developments, each item dated | "This week" |
| D | Clearly labelled evergreen educational explainer (reserve `content/evergreen/`, 14 items) | "EDUCATIONAL EXPLAINER" / "Education edition" |

Take the highest tier that has one verified item and stop researching. **A lack of domestic announcements is never a reason to cancel or hold an edition.** One item failing verification drops that item, not the edition. **HOLD (drafts only, owner notified) is reserved for genuine verification, safety, compliance, authorisation or technical issues that cannot be resolved with approved fallback content.** Never repeat the same story in the same day unless materially updated; no educational subject repeats within 21 days.

## 3. Evidence standard
- **Official statistics and policy decisions:** prefer the official source (central banks, statistical agencies, regulators, IMF, World Bank, OECD, IEA, EIA, OPEC, company investor-relations pages, stock-exchange announcements, official service-status pages). Government sources are preferred for statistics and policy decisions but **are not mandatory for every story.**
- **Routine factual reporting:** one credible, accessible, properly attributed news report is sufficient evidence. Attribute inside the text ("Reuters reports…", "the company said in a statement…").
- **Disputed, politically sensitive, consequential or uncertain claims** (wars, sanctions, accusations, casualties, market-moving rumours, leaks, health or safety claims, anything affecting a named person or company's integrity): require **independent corroboration** (a second independent outlet or the official/primary source) before publication. If not corroborated, drop the item and move down the ladder.
- **Never evidence:** anonymous social-media accounts, unverified blogs, sensational headlines, AI-generated summaries (including search-engine AI answers), or text you cannot attribute to a named publisher.
- **Credible journalism authorised:** Reuters, Associated Press, Bloomberg, BBC, Financial Times, CNBC, ABC Australia, Australian Financial Review, SBS, Nikkei Asia, Al Jazeera, Economic Times, Business Standard, Mint, Moneycontrol. Structured list and fetch targets: `content/research/sources.json`.
- Distinguish confirmed facts, expert opinion, forecasts and possible outcomes. Every item states its event date and publication date. Do not bypass paywalls, access restrictions or bot protection; an inaccessible page is recorded `blocked`, never retried in the same run, and the next source is used.

## 4. Explaining why it matters
Where the evidence supports it, add one or two sentences on the relevance to Australia, India, households or businesses (for example a Middle East energy disruption and possible fuel prices; US rate decisions and currency markets; Chinese manufacturing and Australian commodity exports; European decisions and trade; a global technology outage and Australian business operations; AI developments and employment or productivity). Separate **confirmed effects** from **possible future effects** ("could", "may", attributed to who says so). **Do not invent an Australian or Indian connection.** A globally significant story may be published for its own importance.

## 5. Efficient newsroom workflow (credit saving; mandatory)
- **Small start:** read the shared research record; then check a few trusted pages (one or two wire/international sources such as BBC, Al Jazeera, AP, CNBC or Reuters; one Australian source such as ABC; one India source such as Mint or Economic Times; plus the official release for the best candidate). **At most 6 page fetches and 2 web searches per run**; stop as soon as one credible, relevant, verified item is found. Expand only for corroboration of a disputed or consequential claim.
- **No parallel agents, no deep research.** One thread. If the Agent tool is the only fetch tool, use one `web-fetch` call per page (counts toward the 6).
- **Shared research record** (Project file `claude/ledger-daily/research.md`; seeded from `content/research/README.md`): one row per verified item with **headline, event date, publication date, original source link, verified facts, economic relevance, category, publishing history** (editions, channels, post IDs, website, video), plus tier, region, retrieved-at and `access` (ok/blocked). All editions, the website, YouTube topic research and TikTok scripts **read it first and reuse verified rows**; nobody repeats the same research per platform. A verified fact still inside its tier window needs no re-fetch; estimates and fast-moving figures older than 24 hours are rechecked.
- **Preflight once per run; lint the final text once.** Branded topic cards for images (`images/cards/`); no new rendering for ordinary posts. One bounded retry of any failed operation, after checking whether it actually succeeded. Routines run on Sonnet 5.5; a cheaper model only after the owner agrees it does not reduce accuracy.
- **Reserve:** at least 7 unused approved evergreen items at all times (14 on 28 Nov).

## 6. Writing and copyright
- Original summaries in our own words; attribute the original source and link it (Facebook, X and the website; Instagram names the source). No quote over 15 words; never copy entire articles, copyrighted photographs or third-party charts; our own branded graphics or properly licensed media only. No paywall or bot-protection bypass.
- Political neutrality; neutral headlines; no sensationalism, fear, urgency or speculation presented as fact.
- **Public content is factual, informational and educational: no personalised investment plans, no buy/sell/hold calls, no recommendations or rankings of products, funds, stocks, ETFs, super, loans, insurance, gold or crypto, no price targets or forecasts for named securities, no trading, fast-money or get-rich-quick framing.** Reporting an attributed market or company development is news; steering a reader is advice.
- Markets and prices: attributed news statements about a market, commodity or currency movement are allowed ("Brent crude rose, Reuters reported, after…"). No price tables, tickers, live quotes, charts or screenshots, and no Indian securities price data displays (SEBI/data-rights rule R03). Causation is stated only as the source states it.
- Education disclosure on every post; closing "Learn. Understand. Stay Informed."; signature line only where it fits.

## 7. Authorisation, verification and recovery
- Educational explainers: authorised for automatic publishing when every factual, branding and compliance check passes. News tiers A–C: authorised under the 28 Nov 2026 directives and `policy/AUTHORISATIONS.md` when the evidence standard in §3 and the compliance rules in §6 are met.
- Create each channel's post once (`customScheduled` before the publish time, `shareNow` until the series `lastCatchUp`; after it, tier A/B news is not published late: use tier C/D as a late edition). Check duplicates first (±45 min of the series time, series label). `get_post` every post; one bounded recovery on failure; verify the final status.
- Notify the owner only for a genuine technical, factual, compliance or authorisation issue that remains after recovery.

## 8. Platforms and workflows covered
Ledger Morning, Ledger Evening, 3:15 PM education, Instagram, Facebook, X (Buffer, each edition once per channel); public website news and insights (VPS news engine; reuses the research record; private My Ledger data is never touched); YouTube topic research and scripts; TikTok educational and news content (manual packs; owner-approved videos only). **Each platform keeps its own approved publishing frequency**: the three daily image editions on Instagram/Facebook/X; YouTube one English episode and three Hindi Shorts a week in the trial (approved videos only, via the VPS worker, private until approved); TikTok and Reels manual packs for approved videos; WhatsApp status manual. This policy does not change any frequency.
Never access or modify private users' financial information, holdings, savings plans or portfolios.
