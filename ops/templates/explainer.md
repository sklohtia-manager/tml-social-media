# Educational explainer templates (tier D; PUBLISHING_POLICY v3.1)

Authorised for automatic publishing when factual, branding and compliance checks pass. Same professional voice as news: confident, simple, factual. **Label it Education; no automatic disclaimer, no "background, not today's news"** (§6A). The model design is `ops/MODEL_EXPLAINER.md`.

## Choosing the content
1. First choice: the next unused item in `content/evergreen/ready/` (E01–E14; not used in the last 30 days; no subject repeated within 21 days). Use its captions unchanged except the label line below.
2. Otherwise write a fresh explainer on the day's calendar theme (`ops/EDITORIAL_CALENDAR.md`). Facts only if verified per PUBLISHING_POLICY §3 (or read from the research record with their release date); otherwise concept text with no statistics. Hypothetical arithmetic is labelled "Hypothetical example" and checked in code; a derived number is "our calculation".

Pick the topic whose title accurately describes the post.

## Text structure (adapted per channel, never identical)
- **Hook:** one specific line on why it matters. **Facebook (100–200 words):** label line, title, three short paragraphs (definition → how it works → hypothetical example or verified figure with its date), the takeaway, source URL where used, hashtags. **Instagram:** hook, numbered points, takeaway, source named, 5–8 hashtags. **X (≤ 280 characters, real link length):** one idea, label, at most 2 hashtags.
- Signature line "Are you financially ready for tomorrow?" only where the topic fits preparedness or planning ahead (once, second-last body line, with lead-in and closing; with #AreYouFinanciallyReady).
- A specific general-information line is added only where a reader could mistake the post for personal advice (for example tax, super, retirement or loans).

## Checks before publishing (all must pass; if one fails, fix or choose another item)
- [ ] Labelled Education; no "today", "breaking" or "latest"; earlier dates stated naturally.
- [ ] Every figure verified with its date, or labelled hypothetical or our calculation; arithmetic recomputed in code.
- [ ] No advice, recommendation, ranking, product steer, prediction presented as fact, or trading framing; lint clear.
- [ ] Branding: green-and-gold card with the TML logo; accurate card title; alt text without disclaimer wording.
- [ ] X ≤ 280 characters; Facebook and Instagram captions differ; no repeat of a subject used in the last 21 days; no duplicate post today.
