# YouTube and TikTok script template (PUBLISHING_POLICY v3.1)

Same voice as the written posts: a responsible mainstream financial news and education publication. English or Hindi (natural, fluent narration; Codex produces the media in TML Media Studio).
- **Hook (first 3 seconds):** the news or question in one concrete sentence, with the figure and date.
- **Body (20–45 s Short; 2–4 min episode):** what happened (attributed, dated), why it matters to everyday life, confirmed vs possible effects, one takeaway. Source named on screen in a compact lower-third. Our own graphics and licensed media only.
- **Label:** News, Markets, Global Economy, Technology, Energy, Education or Insights, in the title or on-screen kicker.
- **Do not** add a spoken or on-screen disclaimer to ordinary videos, say "not today's news", or apologise for the content. Mention an earlier release's date naturally. Add a specific note only where genuinely needed ("hypothetical example", an attributed forecast, or tax/retirement content that could be read as personal advice).
- **Standing disclosure** lives in the channel description and profile (`brand/PLATFORM_DISCLOSURES.md`).
- Compliance unchanged: verified facts (shared research record), no recommendations, no price targets, no trading framing; owner approval and the video release gate still apply; TikTok stays manual.
