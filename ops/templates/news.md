# News post templates (tiers A–C; global scope; PUBLISHING_POLICY v3)

Original wording, own headline, source named and linked (link on Facebook and X; Instagram names it). Hook first: one specific line on why it matters. No quote over 15 words; no copied text, photos or charts.

## Fields to fill from the research record
Headline · event date · publication date · source(s) · verified facts · economic relevance (confirmed vs possible; Australia/India link only if real) · category · tier.

## Facebook (100–200 words)
`<LABEL>` (THE LEDGER MORNING / THE LEDGER EVENING; add "In case you missed it, <date>" for tier B, "This week" for tier C)
Hook line.
What happened (2–3 sentences, attributed: "Reuters reports…", "the company said…", dates and units).
Why it matters (1–2 sentences; confirmed effects first, then "could"/"may" for possible effects, attributed to who says so).
Source: <publisher>, <publication date>. <URL>
Learn. Understand. Stay Informed. · hashtags (3–5) · "For information and education only. Not financial advice."

## Instagram
Hook, 3–5 short points, a save-worthy takeaway, "Source: <publisher>, <date>.", "Education only. Not financial advice.", 5–8 hashtags (#TwoMarketLedger + edition tag + topic tags).

## X (max 280 characters counting the real link length; check in code)
One fact + one line of relevance + "Source: <publisher>" + link only if it fits; at most 2 hashtags; "Education only, not advice." if room.

## Do not
Recommend or rank products, funds, stocks, ETFs, crypto or gold; give targets or forecasts for named securities; show price tables, tickers, charts or Indian securities prices; imply "buy now"; state causation the source does not state; present a possible outcome as fact; invent a local connection; use fear or urgency; take political sides; bypass paywalls.
