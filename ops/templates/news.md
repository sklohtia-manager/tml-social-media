# News post templates (tiers A–C; PUBLISHING_POLICY v3.1)

Professional mainstream-publication voice: strong headline, simple facts, dates and verified figures, concise attribution, relevance to everyday life. Original wording; source named and linked (link on Facebook and X; Instagram names it). **No automatic disclaimers and no "not today's news" wording** (PUBLISHING_POLICY §6A). Earlier developments carry their original date in the sentence ("The ABS said on 25 November…"); only tier A may say new, latest or today.

**Content label** (first line, and the graphic kicker): News · Markets · Global Economy · Technology · Energy · Education · Insights.

## Fields from the research record
Headline · event date · publication date · source(s) · verified facts · economic relevance (confirmed vs possible; Australia/India link only if real) · category · tier.

## Facebook (100–200 words)
`<EDITION LABEL> | <CONTENT LABEL>` e.g. `THE LEDGER MORNING | GLOBAL ECONOMY`
Strong headline sentence.
What happened (2–3 sentences, attributed, with dates and units).
Why it matters (1–2 sentences: confirmed effects first, then "could"/"may" attributed to who says so).
Source: <publisher>, <date>. <URL>
Hashtags (3–5). Optional closing: Learn. Understand. Stay Informed.

## Instagram
Label line, headline, 3–5 short points, a takeaway, "Source: <publisher>, <date>.", 5–8 hashtags.

## X (max 280 characters counting the real link length; check in code)
Label, one fact with its date, one line of relevance, "Source: <publisher>" and the link only if it fits; at most 2 hashtags.

## Graphic (our own; `tools/render.py`)
Spec: `kicker` (content label), headline that carries the news, `figures` (one or two verified figures with label and period) and/or `chart` (our own simple bar chart from verified numbers), `context` (one or two sentences), compact `source` line with date. Logo and colours unchanged. Hosting limit: scheduled runs cannot upload a custom graphic, so they use the matching topic card; custom graphics are for interactive/VPS publishing (see handover).

## When a specific note is warranted
"Hypothetical example", "our calculation", a forecast attributed to its author, or a short general-information line on a post about tax, super, retirement or loans that could be read as personal advice. Not otherwise.

## Do not
Recommend or rank products, funds, stocks, ETFs, crypto or gold; give targets or forecasts for named securities; show price tables, tickers or Indian securities prices; imply "buy now"; state causation the source does not state; present a possible outcome as fact; invent a local connection; use fear or urgency; take political sides; bypass paywalls; copy text, photos or third-party charts.
