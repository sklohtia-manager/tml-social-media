# The Ledger Morning: 9:30 AM (FE-01)

**Scope:** previous-day events, overnight international developments and fresh morning announcements (Australia, India, United States, global). Prioritise economic, business and government news that affects everyday life:
- rates and inflation;
- cost of living;
- jobs and wages;
- tax and budget;
- rent and housing;
- energy;
- trade and growth;
- AUD/INR/USD and remittances;
- retirement.

Pick the single most important verified story, preferring neutral macro news. If nothing fresh can be verified, publish a labelled "EDUCATIONAL EXPLAINER" (topic card "explainer").

Cover what happened, why it matters (only causes the source states), the potential economic and household impact, and what is uncertain. Write a HEADLINE.

**Instagram**
- Hook line, then short paragraphs.
- Name the source (no links).
- "Education only. Not financial advice."
- 5–12 hashtags, including #TwoMarketLedger #TheLedgerMorning.

**Facebook**
- 100–200 words (count with code), plain language, practical relevance.
- Primary-source URL.
- Close with "For information and education only. Not financial advice."

**X**
- At most 280 characters, links counting as 23 (count with code).
- "Source: <publisher>", "Education only, not advice." and the link if it fits.

**All channels**
- Each platform's text is adapted, never identical.
- The optional "Are you financially ready for tomorrow?" only when it is not fear- or urgency-driven.
- Paraphrase; no quote over 15 words.
- In the log notes, note any major release due later today.
