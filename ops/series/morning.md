# The Ledger Morning: 9:30 AM (FE-01)

**Scope:** previous-day events, overnight international developments and fresh morning announcements (Australia, India, United States, global). Prioritise economic, business and government news that affects everyday life:
- rates and inflation;
- cost of living;
- jobs and wages;
- tax and budget;
- rent and housing;
- energy;
- trade and growth;
- AUD/INR/USD and remittances;
- retirement.

Pick the single most important verified story, preferring neutral macro news (tier A). If nothing fresh verifies within the research budget, move **automatically** down `ops/PUBLISHING_POLICY.md` §1: tier B (a verified development from the previous 24–72 hours, dated, "In case you missed it"), tier C (weekly economic developments), then tier D (a labelled "EDUCATIONAL EXPLAINER" using `ops/templates/explainer.md`, with the topic card that fits the topic). Never hold the edition for lack of news; never present B, C or D as news.

Cover what happened, why it matters (only causes the source states), the potential economic and household impact, and what is uncertain. Write a HEADLINE.

**Instagram**
- Hook line, then short paragraphs.
- Name the source (no links).
- "Education only. Not financial advice."
- 5–12 hashtags, including #TwoMarketLedger #TheLedgerMorning.

**Facebook**
- 100–200 words (count with code), plain language, practical relevance.
- Primary-source URL.
- Close with "For information and education only. Not financial advice."

**X**
- At most 280 characters, links counting as 23 (count with code).
- "Source: <publisher>", "Education only, not advice." and the link if it fits.

**All channels**
- Each platform's text is adapted, never identical.
- "Are you financially ready for tomorrow?" is an approved signature line, optional: use it only when the topic naturally connects to preparedness, resilience or planning ahead, once per piece, near the end as the second-last line with a lead-in and a short closing (see `ops/series/ready.md`, "Signature line"). News recaps normally close on their own takeaway.
- Paraphrase; no quote over 15 words.
- In the log notes, note any major release due later today.

## Audience-growth standards (owner priority, 27 Nov 2026)
- **Hook first:** start every caption with one factual, specific line that says why it matters to a household, such as "Your $100 now buys about $95 of last year's shopping." No clickbait, questions that mislead, or ALL CAPS.
- **Headline on the card or graphic:** short (≤ 8 words where possible), concrete, and with the number and its period when there is one.
- **Platform fit:**
  - Instagram: hook, 3–5 short paragraphs or numbered points, and a save-worthy takeaway line;
  - Facebook: a slightly longer plain-language explainer with the source link;
  - X: one fact, one line of context and the source link.
- **Hashtags:** 5–8 relevant ones on Instagram, mixing broad (#FinancialLiteracy) and specific (#AustralianEconomy, #RBA, #Inflation); 3–5 on Facebook; at most 2 on X. Never trending-bait.
- **Close** with the educational line "Learn. Understand. Stay Informed." and the disclosure.
- **Consistency:** publish at the approved times every day. A late post is never better than a held one.
- **Referrals:** once the public website is live and approved, add the relevant website page link on Facebook and X.
