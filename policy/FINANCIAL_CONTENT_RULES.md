# Two Market Ledger — mandatory financial content rules
Version 1.1 | 25 November 2026 | Founder review draft (v1.1 adds founder exception FE-01)

## Authority and scope
Apply to Claude, Codex, editors, contractors and all AI-generated output: public and paid pages, private member tools, PDFs, spreadsheets, newsletters, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, captions, thumbnails, hashtags, comments, DMs, translations, advertisements and scheduled posts. These are conservative internal restrictions, not a claim of legal clearance. Do not assume TML holds an AFSL, credit licence or SEBI registration. No exception without documented founder approval and appropriate legal review. If uncertain, HOLD. Never publish automatically, except as permitted by founder exception FE-01 below.

## FE-01 — Founder exception: The Ledger Daily social auto-publishing (hybrid)
Recorded 25 November 2026, 23:15 AEDT. Authorised by the founder (sole approver) in chat. Legal review not yet obtained; this exception does not represent legal clearance.
- Scope: The Ledger Daily morning (9:30 AM) and evening (7:30 PM) editions, Australia/Melbourne time, on the TML Facebook Page, X @TwoMarketLedger and Instagram @twomarketledger via Buffer only. Nothing else (website, newsletter, video, paid content) is covered.
- Automatic publication is permitted ONLY when the edition is classified ALLOW under every rule in this file, and additionally: (a) every factual claim is confirmed against a primary official source (government statistics agency, central bank, regulator, treasury/finance ministry, OECD/IMF/World Bank, or the company's own release/regulatory filing) retrieved during the same run; (b) no named-security prices, share-price moves, index levels, price targets, forecasts for named securities or Indian securities price data; (c) no product, fund, lender, insurer, super fund, gold or crypto mention that could steer readers; (d) no unsupported causation, rumours or reliance on unverified reports; (e) the education disclosure is included.
- Anything classified BLOCK, or an individual item where verification is incomplete, is not published and is dropped or rewritten. **Amended by the owner on 28 Nov 2026:** an edition is not held merely because fresh news is unavailable; the run moves down the content ladder (`ops/PUBLISHING_POLICY.md` §1) to a verified older development, weekly summary or educational explainer. A genuinely uncertain item is dropped (never published); where nothing can be published, drafts are saved for the founder.
- The founder may edit or remove published posts. Errors follow R08 step 6 (suspend, preserve evidence, correct, record).
- Every run records its classification, sources and Buffer post IDs in claude/ledger-daily/log.md.
- The founder may revoke FE-01 at any time; revocation takes effect immediately.

## R01 — Education only
ALLOW neutral, accurate explanations of budgeting, savings arithmetic, CPI, wages, rent, housing costs, economic history, financial terminology and generic product mechanics with balanced limitations. Do not steer people toward or away from a financial product or product class. General advice is still advice in Australia; free content, a paywall, a disclaimer or an overseas audience does not provide immunity. Do not rely on media exemptions without counsel.

## R02 — Prohibited outputs
BLOCK buy/sell/hold calls, top funds, rankings, model portfolios, asset allocation, price targets, entry/exit signals, forecasts for named securities, personalised product suggestions, implied endorsements, guaranteed returns, risk-free claims, urgency or fear-driven selling. No broker/lender referral fees, affiliate links, sponsored product rankings, trade execution, account-opening assistance or lead routing. Do not recommend specific loans, insurance, super funds, gold products or crypto. No personalised tax, migration or legal advice. Do not use coded names, screenshots, emojis or rhetorical questions to evade rules.

## R03 — India-specific data controls
SEBI circular 8 May 2026, effective 1 July 2026, prescribes 30-day lag for sharing/usage of price data for educational purposes within its scope; it modifies the 2024 sharing and 2025 education rules. The amended FAQ addresses recent security prices used to indicate future prices/advice; the sharing provision requires appropriate agreements, safeguards and audit trails and no monetary incentive to participants. NISM simulation exception is not for TML. Internal policy: block public/member Indian securities price displays, including widgets, tickers, charts and screenshots, unless written legal and data-rights clearance is recorded; for approved education data enforce at least 30 days' lag (longer if required). Do not assume this rule covers every economic statistic or that delay alone grants permission. Neutral current macroeconomic news may be drafted from official sources. Paid membership does not exempt content. SEBI regulated entities and agents have separate social-media identity disclosure obligations; do not imply registration or use regulator logos.

## R04 — Data rights and source integrity
For every factual claim record source URL, publisher, observation period, publication date, retrieval date, exact metric/unit and limitations. Use ABS, RBA, RBI, official statistics and original company releases where relevant; confirm commercial reuse rights. ASX delayed redistribution needs an appropriate agreement; check index owner and vendor rights independently. Public access, API availability and personal broker subscriptions do not grant redistribution. No scraping behind login. Maintain a rights register with permitted channel, purpose, territory, delay, expiry, attribution and approval. Fail closed if missing or stale; display unavailable, not fabricated numbers. Distinguish actual data, sample data, scenarios and forecasts. Verify breaking news immediately before approval; no unsupported causation or rumours.

## R05 — Calculators and private tools
Use transparent arithmetic and user-selected assumptions; show units, formula, limitations, rounding, date and sensitivity. No recommended/default investment product, allocation or return presented as expected; prefer zero-return savings examples. No loan approval/eligibility, specific lender suggestions, tax conclusions or automated suitability scores. User-entered financial data stays private; no export to public analytics, logs or AI prompts. Founder-only personal fund research must remain isolated from all public/free/paid member accounts, search, APIs, cached pages, exports and fixtures. Do not delete founder data; quarantine public access. Test server-side access, cross-account isolation and data export/deletion.

## R06 — Commercial content
Sell education, workbooks and software convenience only. Do not sell recommendations, regulated advice or private fund rankings. No financial-product sponsorship or affiliate programme without separate legal approval. Other sponsorships require due diligence, clear prominent advertising disclosure and editorial independence. Accurately state price, inclusions, limitations, renewal/cancellation and availability; no fake testimonials, misleading scarcity or blanket no-refund claims. No activation of checkout, subscriptions, emails or public registration without owner approval and applicable review.

## R07 — Social media and editorial examples
ALLOW draft: 'Inflation reduces what a fixed amount of money can buy. What expenses have changed in your household?' Source actual statistics if used.
ALLOW draft: 'A $60,000 target less $10,000 already saved leaves $50,000. Time depends on your savings rate and changing costs.'
HOLD: 'Nvidia fell after its CEO resigned.' Verify the alleged resignation, exact dates, data rights and causal evidence; do not publish unverified claims or infer buy opportunities. Prefer neutral broad economic reporting.
BLOCK: 'Gold is the safest investment during war'; rewrite to explain uncertainty and different risks without recommending gold.
BLOCK: 'Best Indian funds for NRIs'; rewrite as neutral explanation of fund terminology and official eligibility resources.
BLOCK: 'Recession is coming, buy now'; rewrite as sourced discussion of scenarios and uncertainty, without a product call.
DM response: 'We provide factual education and planning tools, not individual financial product recommendations. For advice specific to you, consult an appropriately authorised professional in your jurisdiction.' Never solicit account details.

## R08 — Mandatory editorial gate
1. Identify audience/jurisdiction, channel, content type, author and version.
2. Check every assertion, source, date, image licence, data rights, claims and overall impression (including visual, audio, caption, CTA and translation).
3. Classify ALLOW FOR REVIEW / HOLD FOR LEGAL OR DATA REVIEW / BLOCK. ALLOW is not permission to publish (except under FE-01).
4. Store evidence and review record: content ID, draft hash/version, claims/source URLs, data rights IDs, conflicts/sponsor disclosures, classification, reviewer, date, decision and corrections history.
5. Human editor approves final exact version; founder separately authorises publication. Any edit invalidates approval. No automatic AI publication or scheduled release without approval, except The Ledger Daily social editions under FE-01. Keep all other publishing disabled during pre-launch.
6. If an error is discovered, suspend affected content, preserve evidence, notify founder, correct prominently and record the correction. For comments/DMs use approved neutral responses; do not provide recommendations.

Implement fail-closed publication gate and role permissions server-side; automated lint is only a warning, never legal approval. Report findings and remediation on a separate branch; no merge/deploy without approval. Keep legal review and data permissions current; recheck before release, quarterly and on regulatory changes. Record unresolved issues rather than guessing.

## Suggested disclosure (not a legal shield)
Two Market Ledger provides factual financial education and illustrative planning tools only. We do not provide investment recommendations, personalised financial, credit, tax or legal advice. Examples use stated assumptions and are not forecasts or guarantees. Seek appropriately authorised professional advice for your circumstances.
