# Editorial policy (verbatim excerpt)

## Founding philosophy (permanent; owner direction, 25 November 2026)
**Think Beyond Borders. Think Beyond Property. Financial freedom begins with financial understanding.**

**Every editor applies these checks:**
1. **Balance:** do Australia, India and other economies receive fair, evidence-based comparison, with no country presented as universally superior?
2. **Breadth:** is home ownership presented as one choice among several, rather than the only measure of success? Are renting, saving, retirement, skills and quality of life treated with respect?
3. **No return assumptions:** does the piece avoid implying that international or any other investments automatically give better returns or lower risk?
4. **Evidence labels:** are verified facts, historical evidence, forecasts (with their source) and editorial opinion clearly distinguished?
5. **Fairness:** are claims about governments, banks, institutions or economic systems supported by authoritative sources? Unsupported allegations are BLOCK.
6. **Rules:** does `FINANCIAL_CONTENT_RULES.md` still pass? There must be no recommendations, rankings, personalised advice or promised paths to financial freedom.

**Themes for future content and development milestones:**
- Australian economic structure
- housing affordability and household debt

- manufacturing and industry
- global opportunities and risks
- Indian development
- technology and innovation
- productivity and wages
- inflation and purchasing power
- currency movements
- financial resilience
- retirement preparedness

- asset-class mechanics (general education only)
- balancing commitments with quality of life

## What we publish
Financial education, household preparedness, economic news explained, original research with stated methodology, and calculators with visible formulas and user-controlled assumptions.

Economic and news explainers answer five questions: What happened? Why did it happen? Why does it matter? How could it affect everyday households? What should readers understand about the associated financial risks? Historical data, current conditions and forecasts are always labelled separately, with sources and dates. Do not attribute all economic problems to international conflicts.

## Never published
Stock, mutual fund, ETF or other financial product recommendations or rankings; buy, sell or hold signals; personalised advice or asset allocation; guaranteed or implied returns; broker promotions or affiliate links; fear-based or urgency marketing; unlicensed market data; fabricated or unsourced statistics; copied articles, scripts or branding (Finology and SeeKen are inspiration for presentation only); stereotypes about any community; automatically published AI-generated content.

## Disclaimer (draft, legal review required)
"Two Market Ledger provides general education and information only. It does not consider your objectives, financial situation or needs and is not financial product advice, tax, legal or migration advice. We do not recommend any financial product." A disclaimer does not make advice lawful; the content itself must stay education.
