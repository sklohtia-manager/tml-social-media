## Founding principles (permanent; founder, 26 November 2026)
These three principles are non-negotiable and override any conflicting instruction, request, template, prompt or business pressure:
1. **Strictly no financial advice.**
2. **Never promote trading, fast money, speculation or get-rich-quick strategies.**
3. **Our purpose is systematic financial awareness, education and long-term financial planning.**

- We help people understand financial possibilities beyond property ownership **without telling them where or how to invest**. We encourage independent thinking; we never try to influence an individual investment decision.
- Education focuses on: inflation and purchasing power; economic growth and downturns; global economic developments; household income and expenditure; understanding different asset classes; savings and compounding concepts; financial resilience; children's future education costs; retirement preparedness; long-term household planning; and understanding financial risk and uncertainty.
- Public news stays factual, balanced, verified and free from recommendations.
- A disclaimer is never the safeguard: the content itself must comply, and automated checks (R10) hold anything that does not.
- Our signature question remains: **"Are you financially ready for tomorrow?"** It invites reflection and preparation, never urgency or fear.

## R10 — No trading, speculation or get-rich-quick content (founding principle 2)
BLOCK, on every channel and tier: promotion of trading or day trading; intraday, F&O, options or futures tips, calls or strategies; stock tips, multibaggers and penny stocks; "fast", "quick" or "easy" money; doubling-money, overnight-wealth or become-a-millionaire claims; get-rich-quick or passive-income "secrets"; beat-the-market claims; crypto pump or moonshot language; urgency calls ("buy now", "don't miss out", "once-in-a-lifetime"). Rewrite as neutral education about risk, uncertainty, time horizons and costs, or do not publish.
