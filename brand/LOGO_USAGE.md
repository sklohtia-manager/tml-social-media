# Two Market Ledger — approved logo

`brand/tml-logo-primary.png` (2400 × 984 px, transparent) is the approved primary logo (Option 03, Modern TML Monogram) from the official brand kit in the website repository (`brand/png/tml-logo-primary-light-2400.png`). The Claude Project also holds the founder-approved master as `brand/tml-logo-primary.png.b64`.

Usage rules (verbatim from the Claude Project's brand/LOGO_USAGE.md, approved 25 November 2026):
- Place the logo only on light backgrounds: warm white #F8F7F2 or white. The green letters disappear on forest green #153D2E or charcoal #202622; on dark layouts, put the logo on a warm-white panel or band.
- Keep the proportions. Do not stretch, recolour, rotate, crop, add effects or redraw it.
- Leave clear space around it of at least the height of the "T" crossbar.
- Minimum width: 240 px on a 1080 × 1080 graphic; 360–480 px is typical for a header or footer.
- Brand colours: forest green #153D2E, charcoal #202622, muted gold #B89A55, warm white #F8F7F2.
- Never pair it with regulator logos or third-party marks that suggest endorsement.
