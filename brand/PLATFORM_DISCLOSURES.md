# Standing platform disclosures (owner directive, 28 Nov 2026)

Appropriate general information and education wording lives **in the profile and About text of each platform and on the website**, not repeated in every ordinary post. Profile text is edited by the owner (or Codex on the website); Claude cannot edit platform profiles. A profile disclaimer does not authorise regulated financial advice.

Core statement (long form): "Two Market Ledger provides general financial, economic and business news, information and education for Australian, Indian and global audiences. Nothing we publish is personal financial advice or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any product; it does not take your objectives, financial situation or needs into account. Consider independent professional advice for your circumstances."
